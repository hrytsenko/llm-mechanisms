Major industry trends that bring vendors closer together.

## As of November 2026 (0.2.0)

- **<a href={`${base}/llms/generation/internal-reasoning/`}>Internal reasoning</a>** — On their newest models, all three vendors keep reasoning from earlier turns, either in the client history or on the server.
- **<a href={`${base}/llms/prompting/prompt-caching/`}>Prompt caching</a>** — Anthropic and OpenAI now both offer automatic and explicit breakpoints: Anthropic added automatic breakpoint placement, and OpenAI added explicit breakpoints.
- **Sampling controls** — All three vendors are retiring sampling controls on their newest models: Anthropic rejects them, OpenAI accepts them only with reasoning off, and Google advises removing them.
