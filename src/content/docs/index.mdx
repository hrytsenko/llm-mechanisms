Each mechanism in the glossary meets two checks:

- **Importance** — it is widely recognized across the industry, not tied to one vendor.
- **Adoption** — vendors offer it as a first-class feature, or at least document how to build it.

Each mechanism is tagged with one of two tiers:

- <Badge {...tierBadges.foundational} /> — to consider from day one. They are needed in almost every production application.
- <Badge {...tierBadges.situational} /> — to be aware of from day one. They are no less important, just needed later or in specific cases.
