This guide covers the mechanisms you use to call a model directly over its HTTP API.

## Tendencies

Across these mechanisms, each vendor leans a consistent way: **Anthropic toward explicit control, OpenAI toward options, Google toward multimodal input.** These are tendencies, not rules.

**Anthropic** leaves control to the application: it resends the conversation, marks what to cache, and brings its own retrieval. Safety is the exception: safety classifiers are built in and refuse through a dedicated stop reason.

**OpenAI** offers the most options and leaves the choice to you: implicit or explicit-only caching, schema or grammar-constrained tool inputs, a standalone moderation service, and an offline tokenizer next to the counting endpoint.

**Google** builds around multimodal input, with per-modality token counts and the most images per request. Elsewhere it leaves less to configure: caching is implicit only, and uploaded files expire automatically.
